# Digital-Payment-Executive-Dashboard-

Project Overview
This project features a multi-page interactive dashboard designed to analyze and visualize the performance of a digital payment ecosystem. It tracks key performance indicators (KPIs) like total transaction volume, success rates, and revenue across four major service verticals: Loans, Insurance, Money Transfers, and Bill Recharges. By segmenting data by age groups, payment status, and failure reasons, the dashboard provides actionable insights into user behavior and system reliability. The inclusion of time-series analysis helps in identifying seasonal trends and monthly growth patterns across different financial products.

Strategic Business Questions
Here are the critical business questions your dashboard is designed to answer, categorized by the pages you created:

1. Overall Business Health (Main Dashboard)

Revenue Concentration: Which service vertical (Loans, Insurance, Money Transfer, or Recharges) contributes the most to the total transaction value?

Demographic Targeting: Which age group is driving the highest "Amount Generated," and is our marketing reaching the "Senior" or "Adult" segments effectively?

System Reliability: What is the overall transaction success rate, and what is the primary technical reason (e.g., Server Error vs. User Error) behind payment failures?

2. Insurance Vertical Analysis

Product Performance: Between Car, Term Life, Bike, and Health insurance, which product line has the highest market share by amount?

Customer Retention: How does the "Insurance Type Contribution" fluctuate over a 12-month period—are there specific months where certain insurance types peak?

Age-Specific Needs: Does the "Senior" age group spend more on specific insurance types compared to "Young Adults"?

3. Loan Portfolio Analysis

Loan Preferences: Which loan category (Auto, Mutual Fund, Gold, or Credit Score) has the highest capital disbursement?

Seasonal Lending: Looking at the "Amount vs Loan" trend, which months show a surge in loan applications or disbursements?

Default Risk Indicators: What is the ratio of "Bank Denied" failures in the loan section compared to other services?

4. Daily Utility & Transfers (Money Transfer & Recharge)
   
Transfer Behavior: Do users prefer transferring money via UPI ID, QR Code, or Mobile Number? (This helps in prioritizing UI/UX updates for those features).

Utility Trends: Is the "Electricity Bill" recharge more frequent than "Cable TV" or "DTH," and how does this volume change monthly?

Error Optimization: Why is "Wrong PIN" a significant reason for failure in transfers, and can we implement a better authentication UI to reduce this?
